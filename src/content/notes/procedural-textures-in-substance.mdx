Substance Designer is a powerful tool for creating procedural textures. Unlike hand-painted textures, procedural materials are resolution-independent, highly customizable, and perfect for building material libraries.

## Why Procedural?

- **Scalability**: One graph can generate infinite variations
- **Resolution Independence**: Output at any resolution without quality loss
- **Iteration Speed**: Tweak parameters instead of repainting
- **Consistency**: Maintain a unified art style across your project

## Building a Basic Stone Material

Start with noise nodes as the foundation:

1. **Base Shape**: Use a `Cells` or `Voronoi` noise for the stone pattern
2. **Height Detail**: Layer `Perlin Noise` for surface variation
3. **Edge Wear**: Use `Slope Blur` and `Ambient Occlusion` for realistic edges
4. **Color**: Gradient map with subtle color variation

## Tips for Game-Ready Materials

- Keep your graph clean and well-organized
- Use exposed parameters for artist-friendly controls
- Always test at the target resolution (mobile often uses 512x512 or 1024x1024)
- Consider channel packing for mobile (RGB + A)

Procedural workflows are essential for modern game development, especially when building large open-world environments.
